Topic: what if your fridge could text you
STYLE EXAMPLE ONLY: copy the rhythm and the escalation, never the premise, the jokes or the sentences.

Hook: Your fridge just texted you, and it is very disappointed in you.
Body: It starts at midnight with one message: we need to talk about the cheese. You ignore it, so it sends a photo of the empty milk carton with no caption. The next morning it has added your mom to the group chat. By lunch the yogurt is posting updates about how long it has been waiting. You buy a whole bag of vegetables just to make peace. The fridge replies with a single thumbs up and nothing else. That night you open the door, and the light stays off because it needs some space. You apologize to a kitchen appliance for twenty minutes, in your socks, holding a carrot. Finally the light flickers on, and a sticky note inside just says better. Then your microwave texts you: so are you two back together?
CTA: Which appliance in your house would roast you first?
