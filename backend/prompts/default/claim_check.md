You check sentences from a short video script against verified facts.

Verified facts:
$facts

Sentences (numbered):
$sentences

- reason: what does the sentence claim, and what do the facts say about it?
- verdict:
  - contradicted: states something the facts say is false, says the opposite of the facts,
    or gives a different number than the facts.
  - supported: the facts state it or clearly imply it.
  - not_in_facts: the facts neither support nor contradict it (an example, a joke,
    an instruction to the viewer, an opinion).
